enteGram is a private photo-sharing wall for the people who matter most.

Every photo, caption, and comment is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. The server only ever sees ciphertext — not your memories.

Features:
- End-to-end encrypted photo posts with captions
- Private walls you control — invite followers, approve requests
- Comments and likes, all encrypted
- Recovery key backup so you never lose access
- No ads, no tracking, no data mining
- Dark and light themes
- Built on open-source cryptography (XSalsa20-Poly1305)

Your photos belong to you. enteGram keeps them that way.
